# Defect log

Record observed defects only. Do not mark a retest PASS until it has run.

| ID | Date | Environment/commit | Steps and expected result | Actual result | Severity | Owner/issue | Fix commit | Retest result/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use OPEN, IN PROGRESS, FIXED (awaiting retest), VERIFIED or DEFERRED for defect
status. Use NOT RUN/BLOCKED with a reason when validation cannot be performed.
Never include passwords, access tokens or client data in evidence.
